<h1>Releasery: Automated Changelog Updates</h1>

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="highlight">Overview</span></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Releasery</strong> is a tool designed to automate the process of updating your changelog page. It integrates seamlessly with <strong>GitHub</strong> and <strong>WordPress.org</strong> to provide real-time updates for your software releases.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="highlight">Key Features</span></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Automatic Updates:</strong> The changelog page updates itself automatically when a release is shipped.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Latest Releases:</strong> Displays the newest releases first, ensuring users see the most current updates.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Integration:</strong> Works with both private <strong>GitHub</strong> repositories and <strong>WordPress.org</strong>.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Webhooks:</strong> Instant webhooks for immediate updates.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Drafts for Review:</strong> Supports drafts for user review before final publication.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Lightweight:</strong> The tool is minimal, with a size of just 50 kilobytes.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="highlight">Installation</span></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Releasery is available for free on <strong>WordPress.org</strong>. Install it to start managing your changelog effortlessly.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
