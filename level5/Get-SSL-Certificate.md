<h1>Get SSL Certificate</h1>

<!-- wp:betterdocs/api-tryit {"method":"get","path":"/ssl-certificates/{uuid}","refId":7757} /--><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Returns details for a single SSL certificate addressed by its own UUID. The raw certificate body and private key are never exposed. Requires the <code>read:sites</code> scope.</p>
