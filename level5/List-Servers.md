<h1>List Servers</h1>

<!-- wp:betterdocs/api-tryit {"method":"get","path":"/servers","refId":7757} /--><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Returns a paginated list of all servers in your team. Supports filtering by search term and status. Requires the <code>read:servers</code> scope.</p>
