<h1>Select or Upload Attachments Documentation</h1>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This document outlines the interface for selecting or uploading attachments in the media library.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="highlight">Interface Overview</span></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The dialog consists of two main tabs: <strong>Upload files</strong> and <strong>Media Library</strong>. The current view is within the <strong>Media Library</strong> tab.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="highlight">Filters</span></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>At the top of the media library, there are options to filter the items:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Filter by type:</strong> Options include "All media items".</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Filter by date:</strong> Options include "All dates".</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="highlight">Search Functionality</span></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A search bar labeled <strong>Search media</strong> is available for quick access to specific items within the library.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
