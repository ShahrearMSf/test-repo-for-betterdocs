<h1>Unban IP Address</h1>

<!-- wp:betterdocs/api-tryit {"method":"delete","path":"/servers/{uuid}/fail2ban/banned-ips/{ip}","refId":7757} /--><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Unbans a single IP address via fail2ban. Runs synchronously over SSH. Requires the <code>write:servers</code> scope and the <code>server:manage-fail2ban</code> permission.</p>
