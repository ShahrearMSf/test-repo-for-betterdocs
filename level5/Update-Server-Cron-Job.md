<h1>Update Server Cron Job</h1>

<!-- wp:betterdocs/api-tryit {"method":"put","path":"/servers/{uuid}/cron-jobs/{cronJobUuid}","refId":7757} /--><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Updates a server-level cron job and reinstalls it on the server. Requires the <code>write:servers</code> scope and the <code>server:manage-cron-jobs</code> permission.</p>
